Visual identity for Paris
fashion house Loewe

Overview

A comprehensive visual identity redesign for the renowned Parisian fashion house, Loewe. The objective was to modernize the brand's aesthetic while honoring its rich heritage in leather craftsmanship. The new system spans across digital platforms, print collateral, and retail environments, introducing a refined typographic language and a minimalist structural approach that elevates the product.

CLIENT Loewe Paris
YEAR 2024
ROLE Art Direction, Identity Design, Web Design
DESCRIPTION Complete brand overhaul including bespoke typography and packaging systems.
